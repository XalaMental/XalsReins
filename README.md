# Xal's Reins

A mount to-do list that shows what you're missing, how to get it, and every
step in between. Works on Retail and on WoW: Forever.

The mount journal tells you what you own. It's far less helpful when what you
actually want to know is what you're missing on the character you're standing
on right now, and what it takes to go get it. Xal's Reins opens straight to
that answer: the mounts you don't own that this character can really get, with
the in-game source for each one and a step-by-step chain for the ones that need
more than just showing up.

## Features

- **The tracker** — a to-do list of mounts you don't own that your character
  can actually get. It shrinks as you collect.
- **Tabs** — All, Zone (what you can get where you're standing), WIP (every
  mount you've started, as a card showing how far along you are), Raids and
  Dungeons.
- **Mount details as cards** — one for where the mount comes from and one for
  each step. Quest steps say where to start.
- **Steps that check themselves off** — quests, reputation, currency, level and
  gold, checked live against your character.
- **Set Waypoint** on vendor mounts — a pin on your map, or an arrow in TomTom
  if you use it.
- **A zone icon** that only shows up, glows and counts while your zone has a
  mount you can get. Click it to open the Zone tab. Show or hide it, make it
  bigger or smaller, or put it back where it started from the settings.
- **Six skins** — Crimson, Ember, Bronze ledger, Midnight, Indigo slate and a
  light Parchment. Pick one in settings and every window changes right away.
- **A real settings window** — a sidebar with Home, Zone alert, Minimap and
  Skin, and one card for each setting.
- **Search your whole collection**, and a separate window for the mounts you own.
- **Trading Post aware** (Retail) — flags mounts that are only purchasable this
  month.
- **Mount rarity** (Retail) — what percent of players own each mount.
- **WoW: Forever** — every mount with its level, gold, reputation and quest
  steps, including the Felsteed, Dreadsteed and Charger chains, the Black
  Qiraji Battle Tank, the Pack Kodo and the Galestriders. Every mount says how
  you get it, only mounts that are really in Forever are shown, and retired
  mounts are marked as such.
- **Works with Xal's Keep** — if you have it installed, Reins opens inside the
  Keep and the Keep takes over the minimap button and zone icon.

The mount list is read live from the game's own mount journal, so it can't go
out of date.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/xro` | Show or hide the mount tracker |
| `/xro collection` | Open your collected mounts |
| `/xro options` | Open the settings window |
| `/xro find <name>` | Look up a mount and how to get it |
| `/xro sources` | Mounts grouped by how they're obtained |

## Installing

Install from CurseForge, or drop the `XalsReins` folder into
`World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\` (Retail) or
`World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\` (WoW: Forever). The same folder
works for both.
